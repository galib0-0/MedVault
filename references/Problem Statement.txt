In India and globally, patient medical records remain fragmented across multiple hospitals, clinics, and diagnostic centers with no unified access mechanism, leading to delayed diagnoses, medication errors, and critical information gaps during medical emergencies. Citizens, particularly those with low medical literacy, are unable to interpret their own reports, while doctors treating unconscious or unresponsive patients lack access to complete medical history. No existing platform simultaneously addresses lifelong health record consolidation, verified emergency doctor access, and AI-powered multilingual medical report interpretation for citizens of all age groups
