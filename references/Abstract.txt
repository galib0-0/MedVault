The rapid digitization of healthcare services has exposed critical gaps in patient medical record management, particularly in developing nations like India where health data remains fragmented across multiple providers, exists predominantly in paper form, and is inaccessible during emergencies. This paper proposes MedVault AI, a centralized cloud-based digital healthcare record management platform integrated with Artificial Intelligence and Natural Language Processing for intelligent medical document analysis. The system enables citizens to securely store, organize, and retrieve their complete medical history — including laboratory reports, prescriptions, diagnostic images, vaccination records, and discharge summaries — under a single unified platform linked to a unique identifier or government-issued identity such as Aadhaar. A dual-portal architecture provides separate authenticated interfaces for patients and licensed medical practitioners, with verified emergency access enabling doctors to retrieve critical patient information when patients are unconscious or unable to communicate. A key innovation of the system is its AI-powered multilingual summarization engine, which processes uploaded medical documents using OCR and NLP techniques to generate simplified, jargon-free health summaries in the user's preferred regional language, highlighting abnormal findings, medication details, and follow-up recommendations. The platform further incorporates pediatric profile management for newborns and minors under parental supervision. Built on a secure microservices architecture with AES-256 encryption, role-based access control, and full audit logging, MedVault AI addresses healthcare accessibility, emergency response, and medical literacy gaps simultaneously, contributing toward a smarter, more inclusive, and patient-centric digital health ecosystem.

Keywords: Electronic Medical Records, Healthcare Informatics, Artificial Intelligence, Natural Language Processing, Multilingual Summarization, Emergency Healthcare Access, Role-Based Access Control, Cloud Computing, Digital Health, Aadhaar Integration
